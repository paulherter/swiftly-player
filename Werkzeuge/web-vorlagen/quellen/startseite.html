Questions.

Is it free?

Yes. Every feature, for everyone. No ads, no account with us.

Do I need my own server?

Yes. Swiftly plays from your Jellyfin server (10.8 or newer), Plex or Emby.

Plex and Emby?

Both work and are still in beta. Plex signs in with a code or QR.

Does my server have to convert anything?

No. Swiftly plays the original. Less work for your server.

Which devices?

iPhone, iPad, Apple TV, Mac, Android, Android TV, Windows and Linux.

Do you collect data?

No. No tracking. Your progress only goes to your own server.
