Get Swiftly on Android and Android TV.

The same app for your phone and your TV. Free, no account with us, and your server stays yours.

Swiftly Player is on Google Play.

Open Google Play

Android phones, tablets and Android TV, Android 9 or newer.

  1. 1

    Install from Google Play

    Tap Install. Updates arrive through Google Play like for any other app.

  2. 2

    Add your server

    Type the address of your Jellyfin server and sign in. Or choose Quick Connect and approve the code from a device that is already signed in.

  3. 3

    Press play

    Your library shows up. The file plays as it is, through libVLC, so MKV, HEVC, AV1 and PGS subtitles need no transcoding on the server.

Android TV

Search for Swiftly in the Play Store on the TV. Or install it from Google Play on your phone and pick the TV under “Install on more devices”. Sign in on the TV with Quick Connect, so you don’t have to type a password with the remote.

Questions

Does it cost anything?

No. All the features you need are free, for everyone, and there is no account with us. Supporters get a few extras on top.

Which devices does it run on?

Android phones and tablets and Android TV, Android 9 or newer. Fire TV is not supported: it installs apps from the Amazon Appstore, not from Google Play.

I joined the test earlier. Do I need to do anything?

No. The app stays installed and keeps updating through Google Play. If you only want the regular releases, leave the test on the testing page.

Is it safe?

It comes from Google Play, like any other app. Your watch progress goes to your own Jellyfin server, nowhere else. The code is on GitHub.
