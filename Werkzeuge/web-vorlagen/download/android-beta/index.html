The beta on Android.

The next build, a week or two before it reaches Google Play. It can break; that is the point, and your report is what fixes it.

The beta is open, no invite code needed. You need a Google account on the device.

Join the beta

Android phones, tablets and Android TV. Open the link on the device you want it on.

  1. 1

    Open the link

    Open the link above on the device, signed in with the Google account you use in Google Play. On a TV, open it on your phone with the same account.

  2. 2

    Tap Join, then install

    Tap Join, wait a minute, then open Swiftly Player in Google Play and tap Install or Update. The beta replaces the normal version and keeps your servers.

  3. 3

    Tell me what broke

    Write on Discord or open an issue on GitHub. Say what you did, what you expected and what happened. The version number is in Settings.

Good to know

  • Leaving is one tap. Open the same link and tap Leave. You then get the normal version again with the next update.
  • Want the stable one? It is on Google Play.

Tell me what you think
