Jellyfin client · Apple · Windows · Linux

It opens, it finds your library, it plays.

Swiftly for Jellyfin is a native Jellyfin client for iPhone, iPad, Apple TV, Mac, Linux and Windows — one app, six platforms, and it never makes your server transcode.

TestFlight Windows Linux

Free, no account. Needs your own Jellyfin server, 10.10 or newer.

Features

Picture in Picture

Keep watching while you do something else. iPhone and iPad.

Resume

Picks up where you stopped, on any device.

Next episode

Starts on its own. Skip intro and credits where your server knows them.

Quick Connect

A six-digit code in a browser. No password on a remote.

No account, no analytics

Nothing leaves your device but the requests to your own server.

Playback

No transcoding. Your server sends the file, Swiftly plays it.

MKV · HEVC 10-bit · TrueHD 7.1 · PGS subtitles Direct Play
MKV · H.264 · DTS-HD MA · SRT subtitles Direct Play
AVI · MPEG-4 Part 2 · MP3 Direct Stream
